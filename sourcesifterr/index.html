Questions

In Case Your Wondering...

Does it work on the stores I already use?

Yes. SourceSifterr rides along in your toolbar and captures from any store you browse: vendors, marketplaces, and brand sites alike.

Where does my data live?

In your own Google Sheet and your own deck. Captures go straight into files you own and control. Nothing is locked inside another platform.

Do I need to know spreadsheets or design software?

No. Set the project, budget, and categories once, then click to capture. The tracker and deck build themselves.

Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes, buy once, own for life. No subscription. Studios and teams can also request a custom build tailored to their workflow.

What browser does it run in?

SourceSifterr is a Chrome extension. Install it, connect your Google account, and you're capturing in minutes.

Can it keep me on budget?

That's the point. Every capture counts down against the budget you set, and your overage lights up red the moment a pick pushes you past your grand total.

Can I add a product manually?

Yes. Alongside one-click page capture, you can add products manually right inside the extension, so anything you can't capture from a page still lands in your tracker and deck.

Can I find it in the Chrome Web Store?

Not yet. SourceSifterr is available by direct purchase only for now, with plans to list it in the Chrome Web Store down the road. Payment is accepted via Venmo, PayPal, or ACH transfer.

Do I need to have a Google account?

At this time, SourceSifterr is only available for users with a Google account, with plans to extend to Microsoft accounts in the future.

I can't find an answer to my question.

Reach out anytime at support@ercoleautomations.com for questions and any additional support you need.
